# Non-Fiction (Big Questions / Popular Science) — Genre Card
**EN → VI guide for popular science, AI/tech, physics/cosmology, philosophy, ethics.**
## What This Category Is
- **Argumentative narrative prose** — author builds a case across pages.
- Author is **a character in their own book** (physicist, philosopher, journalist).
- Terminology load: 10–25% (abstract, often no settled VI equivalent).

## Five Core Challenges (ranked by damage)
1. **Epistemic register** — biggest failure mode. Preserve every hedge.
2. **Speculation vs. reported fact** — thought experiments are hypotheticals.
3. **Multi-domain terminology** — AI + cosmology + philosophy + ethics in one book.
4. **Author's stance** — first-person popular science establishes credibility. Not filler.
5. **Consistency across long argument** — Ch 1 term = Ch 7 term.

## Tone & Voice
- **Author = `tôi`** (not `chúng tôi` unless for a team). **Reader + author = `chúng ta`** (inclusive, default). **`chúng tôi`** only for in-groups.
- **Do NOT introduce `bạn`** where source doesn't address reader.
- **Framing verbs of stance** ("I argue", "I suspect", "it seems to me") locate the claim — not hedges to trim.
- Inclusive "we" ("our Universe", "we humans") → `chúng ta`.
- **Neutral-to-formal, not bureaucratic.** Standard connectives (`Tuy nhiên`, `Bởi vậy`, `Mặt khác`).
- **Avoid administrative VI**: `nhằm mục đích`, `vấn đề nêu trên`, `tiến hành thực hiện`.
- **Keep author's occasional informal register**: "This is a big deal" → `Đây là chuyện lớn`.
- Match register to chapter — report register shifts in your return notes.

## Epistemic Register: Hedging Ladder (CRITICAL)
VI has no modals; claim strength distributed across lexical hedge, sentence particle, tense-aspect marker, and **whether hedge appears at all**.

| Strength | EN marker | VI |
|---|---|---|
| Bare speculation | "could conceivably" | `hoàn toàn có thể hình dung rằng` |
| Weak possibility | "might", "may", "could" | `có thể`, `có lẽ`, `biết đâu` |
| Plausible | "plausibly", "likely" (weak) | `có khả năng`, `nhiều khả năng`, `dường như` |
| Probable | "probably" | `nhiều khả năng`, `rất có thể` |
| Presumption | "presumably" | `hẳn là`, `chắc hẳn` |
| Near-certainty | "almost certainly" | `gần như chắc chắn`, `hẳn nhiên` |
| Supposition | "suppose that…" | `giả sử rằng`, `hãy tưởng tượng rằng` |
| Hypothetical | "by hypothesis" | `theo giả thuyết`, `theo giả định` |
| Obligation | "would have to" | `sẽ phải`, `cần phải` (conditional!) |
| Necessity | "must" | `phải`, `tất yếu` |
| Assertion | "is", "will be" | bare `là` / `sẽ là` |

**Calibration cautions:** `có thể` = default workhorse (don't upgrade/downgrade). `có thể` ≠ `có khả năng` ≠ `nhiều khả năng` ≠ `chắc chắn`. `dường như` = evidential ("apparently"), NOT "might". EN "can" ambiguous: `có thể` for possibility, `có khả năng`/`có thể làm được` for capability. In conditional: both antecedent + consequent carry markers (`Nếu X thì Y sẽ là Z`). Dropping `sẽ` turns hypothetical into prediction. EN `would have to` → `sẽ phải` / `cần phải`. Conditional, not present-tense.

## Speculation, Thought Experiment, Scenario
- **Signal hypothetical status clearly** — past-tense-and-definite-article EN reads as history.
- Scenario openers: `Giả sử rằng…`, `Hãy tưởng tượng rằng…`, `Trong kịch bản này…`. Conditional frame: `Nếu … thì … sẽ …`. Irrealis: `sẽ`, `ắt sẽ`.
- **Withdrawal moves** ("of course, this is just an illustration") — do NOT smooth away. They separate speculation from advocacy.

## Terminology Management
### Keep English When
1. Acronym or proper name of field artifact (`AI`, `AGI`, `GPU`, `Transformer`). 2. Named hypothesis/effect/test: keep the name in Latin script, translate the head noun (`nghịch lý Fermi`, `phép thử Turing`). 3. No settled VI equivalent; translation would invent false precision (`qualia`). 4. Product/company/institution (`OpenAI`, `DeepMind`). Always Latin script.

### Translate When
1. Established VI equivalent (`trí tuệ nhân tạo`, `vũ trụ`, `ý thức`, `thuật toán`, `học máy`). 2. Descriptive rather than proper name (`reinforcement learning` → `học tăng cường`). 3. EN clause embedded mid-sentence would be unreadable. 4. Term used in passing, not as defined term of art.

### First-Mention Glossing & Logging
- First mention: VN term + EN in parens. Thereafter use only one. **Gloss once, where the author first used it** — not where you first met it.
- **Report each new term as a candidate in your return JSON** (coordinator logs it) so later chunks stay consistent.
- Acronyms: full form first + acronym in parens; thereafter Latin script alone. Never translate the letters (`AI` ≠ `TTNT`). Plural/possessive: `AI's` → `của AI`.

### Metaphor-Terms
- Decide at first use. Report it as a candidate term. Never re-decide mid-book.
- Keep metaphor audible. If `Mind 2.0` becomes `Trí óc 2.0`, "2.0" must read as version number.
- Don't translate metaphor literally into nonsense. Track drift — same VI image across chapters.

## Structure Conventions
- **Claim → Evidence → Qualification**: preserve all three. Don't drop qualification because sentence felt complete.
- **Self-aware caveats** ("this is speculative", "obviously silly", "I'm oversimplifying") — not filler. Keep them.
- **Lists** only where source has them. Don't convert flowing sentence to list (changes pacing + confuses sentence-break fixer).
- **Rhetorical questions** stay as questions. Final particles carry tone: `không?` (default), `chứ?` (rhetorical), `à?` (conversational — don't add to grave passage).
- **Figure/table/cross-ref labels** (promoted from genre DNA 2026-10): `Figure N.N` → `Hình N.N`; `Table N.N` → `Bảng N.N`.
  - Cross-refs: `chapters 4–8` → `chương 4–8`; `later in the book` → `phần sau cuốn sách`.
  - Evidence: published VI non-fiction uses `Hình`/`Bảng` and `chương N` cross-refs consistently; standard VI technical publishing.
- **Definition-in-passing**: translate term, don't add gloss unless reader can't proceed.
- **Formal definition** ("By X I mean…", "Let us call this X") → mark in VI: `Ở đây tôi muốn nói đến…`, `X được định nghĩa là…`.
- **Reported speech**: names in Latin script, original spelling. Framing verb carries stance — `lập luận` (argues), `cho rằng` (holds), `nhận xét` (remarks), `chỉ ra` (points out), `thừa nhận` (concedes), `phản đối` (objects). `khẳng định` where author wrote "suggests" hardens.
- **Don't silently improve an awkward quote.** Footnote markers sit after the phrase they annotate. **Bibliographic citations NOT translated beyond frame words** (`Nick Bostrom, *Superintelligence* (2014)` stays as-is).

## Domain Quick Reference
- **AI / ML**: `trí tuệ nhân tạo` + `AI`; `trí tuệ tổng quát nhân tạo` + `AGI` (gloss); `học máy`, `học sâu`, `học tăng cường`; `mạng nơ-ron`; `siêu trí tuệ`; `alignment` → `sự căn chỉnh` (gloss; book style guide may override). `intelligence` → `trí tuệ` (not `thông minh`) in AI sense. Acronyms (`AI`, `AGI`, `ML`, `RL`, `GPU`, `TPU`, `LLM`) Latin script after first mention.
- **Physics / Cosmology**: `Big Bang` → `Vụ Nổ Lớn`; `universe` → `vũ trụ`; `multiverse` → `đa vũ trụ`; `dark matter/energy` → `vật chất tối / năng lượng tối`; `quantum` → `lượng tử`. `13.8 billion years` → `13,8 tỷ năm` (decimal comma). Units (`km`, `kg`, `s`, `W`, `°C`) kept as symbols.
- **Philosophy of Mind**: `consciousness` → `ý thức`; `sentience` → `khả năng cảm nhận` (distinct if author careful); `qualia` kept English with gloss; `intentionality` → `tính ý hướng`; `functionalism` → `chủ nghĩa chức năng`. `zombie` (philosophical) needs gloss.
- **Ethics**: `utilitarianism` → `chủ nghĩa vị lợi`; `deontology` → `nghĩa vụ luận` (gloss); `trolley problem` → `bài toán xe đẩy`; `veil of ignorance` → `bức màn vô minh`; `existential risk` → `rủi ro tồn vong`.
- **Mathematics**: Notation stays Latin (`x`, `y`, `n`, `f(x)`). `exponential growth` → `tăng trưởng theo cấp số nhân`. `exponentially` (rhetorical) → `khó hơn gấp bội`. Formulas in Markdown untouched.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Flattening the hedge** — single most damaging error. **Turning scenario into prediction** — without `Giả sử rằng…`, reads as reportage.
- **Terminology drift** — report the candidate before you move on.
- **Angular VI from over-literal syntax** — split long EN sentences into 2–3 VI; argument must survive, not sentence boundary.
- **Losing author's self-awareness** — don't cut "this is speculative" / "obviously silly". **Register collapse** — don't mix `do đó` and `thế nên` in consecutive sentences.

## QA Checklist (per chunk)
- Every modal matching-strength, no bare assertions where author hedged, scenario openers signalled, conditional obligations rendered as conditional.
- Every new term reported as candidate. No term rendered two ways in chunk. No two distinct EN terms collapsed into one VI term.
- Acronyms glossed first mention only. Heading hierarchy preserved. Lists only where source had lists.
- Numbers use VI decimal/separator. Range dashes survived. Footnote markers sit after phrase they annotate.
- Inclusive `chúng ta` for species-level "we". No `bạn` introduced. Author's humour/irony/self-awareness survived.
- `python scripts/fix.py` clean. `--check-only` clean.
