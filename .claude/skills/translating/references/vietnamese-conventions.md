# Vietnamese Conventions (Layer 1 — Rule Card)

## Quotation Marks
- Double straight: `" "` (not curly). No space inside: `"text"`.
- Single straight for nested quotes: `' '`.
- Punctuation **inside** quotes if part of quoted content; **outside** if not.
- Multi-line block: quotes only at start and end of entire block.
- Dialogue: colon intro `Anh nói: "..."`. Keep source block boundaries; never split or merge blocks for dialogue.

## Dashes (project hard rule)
- **No em-dashes (`—`).**
- Prose parenthetical: spaced ASCII hyphen ` - `. Dialogue lines: leading `- `.
- Numeric ranges: unspaced en-dash `9–10`, `10–20%`. `10–20` must NOT become `10 - 20`.
- Preserve lexical hyphens in compounds: `self-aware`.

## Ellipsis & Punctuation Spacing
- Three dots: `...` (not Unicode `…`).
- Single space after punctuation (not double). Punctuation immediately follows word.
- Colon: one space after, introduces explanation/list/quote.

## Numbers
- Thousands separator **dot**, decimal **comma**: EN `1,000.50` → VI `1.000,50`; `1.000.000`; `0,75`.
- Always `0` before decimal for numbers < 1: `0,5` not `,5`.

## Dates & Time
- **DD/MM/YYYY**, leading zeros on day/month. Written: `ngày [day] tháng [month] năm [year]`. Days: `Chủ nhật`, `thứ hai` … `thứ bảy`.
- March 4, 2011 → `4 tháng 3 năm 2011`. The 2010s → `thập niên 2010`. The 21st century → `thế kỷ 21`.
- Time: 12-hour common (`09:30`/`9:30`), AM/PM optional; 24-hour where context demands.

## Currency
- VND `₫`/`đ` **after** the number: `1.000.000 đ`.
- Foreign codes (USD, GBP, EUR) uppercase, not translated, before amount: `100 USD` (not `100 đô la Mỹ`).

## Capitalization Rules
- **Proper nouns / geographical names**: capitalize ALL syllables — `Hà Nội`, `Nguyễn Du`, `Bắc Trung Bộ`.
- **Geographical + common noun**: capitalize proper, lowercase common — `Cửa Lô beach` (not `Cửa Lô Beach`).
- **Book/article titles**: capitalize ONLY first word + proper-noun syllables — `Tìm hiểu về lịch sử Việt Nam`.
- **Journal/newspaper/org names**: capitalize all syllables — `Báo Nhân Dân`, `Tạp Chí Văn Học`.
- **Sentence start**: capitalize first letter.
- **No accents on initial capital letters** when transliterating from non-VN script (`Again`, not `Ágain`).

## Emphasis
- Bold: important terms, headers, book titles. Italic: foreign words, emphasized phrases, titles. Underline: minimal.

## Sentence Structure (Vietnamese)
- **SVO** order. **Topic-comment** when natural: `Sách này thì tôi đọc rồi.`
- **Adjectives AFTER nouns**: `tall man` → `đàn ông cao`.
- **Adverbs AFTER objects** (when object present); never Topic + Adverb + Verb + Object.
- **Tense particles** (not conjugation): `đã`, `đang`, `sẽ`. Often omitted when context is clear.
- VI handles long sentences like EN; break only for readability. **Splitting one EN sentence into 2–3 VI sentences is often correct** — argument must survive, not sentence boundary.

## Formal vs. Informal Register
- **Formal**: full sentences, no slang, formal pronouns (`anh/chị`), `ạ` particle.
- **Informal**: simpler structure, colloquial dialogue, casual pronouns (`tớ/cậu`, `mình`, `bạn`), particles `nha/nhé/mà`.
- Markers: `ạ` (formal/social superior), `nha/nhá` (regional, reduces formality), kinship terms (`anh`/`chị`/`em`/`bạn`/`ông`/`bà`).
- Match register to source. Mix (narrative vs dialogue) only if source mixes.

## Foreign Names & Loanwords
- **Modern convention**: keep original spelling, capitalize each component (`Vladimir Ilich Lenin`, `Kim Il Sung`).
- **Historical figures with established VI equivalents**: use them (`Khổng Tử` for Confucius).
- **Trademarks/brands/modern products NEVER change**: `Coca-Cola`, `Google`, `iPhone`.
- **Established VI term exists** → translate (`Computer` → `máy tính`). Sino-Vietnamese vocabulary is native; treat as such.

## Acronyms
- **Widely-used VI acronyms**: use them (`HLV`, `BTC`, `THPT`).
- **Universal acronyms**: keep as-is (`UNESCO`, `FIFA`, `USA`).
- **No VI equivalent**: keep source acronym; optional full term in parens at first mention.

## Dialogue Tags & Speech
- Verbs: `nói`, `hỏi`, `trả lời`, `gọi`, `yêu cầu`, `lẩm bẩm`, `thì thầm`. After speech with comma, or before with colon.
- **Direct**: `"…"` with intro. **Indirect**: no marks, modify pronouns/tense. Nested quotes `'…'`.

## Measurement Units
- **Metric standard**: `giây`, `mét`, `ki-lô-gam`/`kg`; Celsius without conversion.
- Imperial source: convert with note OR keep original with conversion.

## Block Quotations & Citations
- Quotes at start/end of entire block. Attribution per Chicago Manual of Style.
- **In-text citations**: maintain format, translate source titles only if well-known.
- **Bibliographic citations NOT translated beyond frame words**: cited title stays in original.

## Rhetorical Questions
- Keep as questions. Final particles carry tone: `không?` (default), `chứ?` (rhetorical/checking), `sao?`, `à?` (conversational).
- Do NOT change rhetorical question to statement. Do NOT add `à?` to grave passage.

## Avoid
- Word-for-word translation. Capture essence; use topic-comment structure when natural.
